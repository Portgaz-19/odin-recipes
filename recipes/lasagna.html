Lasagna

Description

This classic homemade lasagna features tender layers of pasta packed with a rich, slow-simmered meat sauce and a creamy three-cheese blend. The savory depth of ground beef or Italian sausage combines beautifully with crushed tomatoes and aromatic herbs like basil and oregano.

Every bite delivers the perfect balance of comfort food textures, from the gooey, melted mozzarella on top to the structured layers underneath. It is an ideal, crowd-pleasing dish for family gatherings, Sunday dinners, or meal-prepping for the week ahead.

Ingredients

  • Meat
  • Aromatics
  • Sauce
  • Seasoning
  • Noodles
  • Cheese blend
  • Topping

Steps

  1. Brown the meat
  2. Simmer the sauce
  3. Mix the cheese
  4. Prep the pan
  5. Layer the ingredients
  6. Repeat layers
  7. Bake covered
  8. Bake uncovered
  9. Rest and serve
