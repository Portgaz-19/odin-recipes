Beef and Broccoli Stir-Fry

Description

This popular takeout dish features thinly sliced beef seared quickly and tossed with crisp-tender broccoli florets in a savory, sweet soy-garlic sauce. The cornstarch coating on the beef ensures it stays incredibly tender and juicy during cooking.

It is a nutritious, high-protein meal that pairs wonderfully with white or brown rice.

Ingredients

  • Vegetables
  • Protein
  • Liquid Sauce
  • Aromatics
  • Thickener
  • Cooking Fat
  • Garnish

Steps

  1. Velvet the beef
  2. Mix the sauce
  3. Steam broccoli
  4. Sear beef
  5. Thicken sauce
  6. Toss and serve
