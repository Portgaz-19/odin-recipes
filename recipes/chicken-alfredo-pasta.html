Chicken Alfredo Pasta

Description

This comforting Italian-American classic features tender chicken breast strips tossed with fettuccine pasta in a rich, velvety cream sauce. Garlic and freshly grated Parmesan cheese provide a savory depth that coats every strand of pasta perfectly.

It is a quick, restaurant-quality weeknight meal that comes together in less than 30 minutes.

  • Pasta
  • Protein
  • Fat
  • Aromatics
  • Sauce
  • Cheese
  • Seasoning

Steps

  1. Cook pasta
  2. Sear chicken
  3. Sauté garlic
  4. Build sauce
  5. Add cheese
  6. Combine and serve
